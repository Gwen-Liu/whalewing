Whalewing adds a personal workbench to DSH: hand work to the assistant and its experts, keep what you learn in your own knowledge base, and handle everyday office files on this computer. Knowledge, skills and settings stay on this machine and are never uploaded. The interface is in Chinese; the names below give the Chinese label you will see.

## Getting started

1. Open 鲸翼 (Whalewing) from the DSH sidebar. Whalewing uses the models you configured in DSH and stores no keys of its own.
2. Click the bubble at the bottom right to open the assistant (小鲸助手) and say what you need. It dispatches the job to a suitable expert and reports back; small things it does itself.
3. The first visit to 知识积累 (Knowledge) creates your knowledge base. Write a few lines in 关于我 (About me); every expert reads it before starting work.
4. The header holds your pinned tabs. Everything else is under 全部功能 (All features); press Ctrl K to search by name or purpose.

## Core features

- **Expert team (专家团队)**: 10 built-in experts (assistant, product, design, development, testing, operations, data, research, writing, marketing) and 10 templates. You can create your own, or let the assistant create one. Each expert keeps its own memory.
- **Assistant (小鲸助手)**: the chief of staff. It triages each request, dispatches specialist work to an expert and follows it to completion, and proposes a new expert when none fits.
- **Knowledge (知识积累)**: your second brain, a local Markdown knowledge base with files and search on the left, tabs for reading and editing, a right panel for outline, backlinks, outgoing links, tags, properties and the local graph, plus a graph of the whole base.
- **Skill Center (技能中心)**: manage the skills, MCP servers and plugin packs your experts can use. Install skills from skills.sh, add MCP servers from 22 verified picks, and install plugin packs. 94 skills are bundled.
- **Scheduled tasks (定时任务)**: a daily memory task turns the day's conversations into a journal and task records, and a weekly review writes the week's notes and open decisions. Both are on by default. You can add your own tasks and assign them to an expert.

## Optional features

These four are on by default. Each can be switched off on the Whalewing page under DSH Plugins; its data is kept.

- **AI web (AI 网页)**: find the right AI website for the job, write a question once, send it to several sites with your background and notes, and save the answers you copy back into your knowledge base.
- **Office workshop (办公工坊)**: give files to the assistant and say what you want. It picks from 44 local operations (conversion, PDF, spreadsheets, images, text, audio and video, QR codes). Nothing is uploaded.
- **Daily reading (每日阅读)**: one issue a day on a single screen. A news brief at 08:00 that can be picked according to your About Me note and saved to your inbox, a daily AI usage tip you can hand to the assistant to try, your local bookshelf with highlights, and a review of past highlights.
- **Model world (模型世界)**: scores and spending for the models you configured, rankings, new releases, a character gallery, duels, and a set of community test questions to check your own models.

Installation, upgrade and extension guides are in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/gwen-liu/whalewing#readme).
